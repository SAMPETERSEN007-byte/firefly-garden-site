The rules, in full

  • Tap an open cell to place a firefly. Tap it again to take it back.
  • A firefly lights its own square and every open square it can see in a straight line — up, down, left and right — until a stone blocks the view.
  • No firefly may see another firefly. If two can see each other, both are marked and the garden will not open.
  • A numbered stone must touch exactly that many fireflies, counting only the four squares directly beside it. A stone with no number is free.
  • The clearing is restored when every open square is lit, every number is exactly satisfied, and no two fireflies can see each other.

Every board has exactly one solution, and each one is proved solvable by ordinary reasoning before it ships. You never have to guess.

Common questions

Is my progress saved? Where?

Yes, automatically, on your device. It is not in the cloud and not tied to an account, so it does not follow you to a new phone — and deleting the app deletes it.

The game said my progress was reset.

Saved data is checked when the app opens. If it is damaged — an interrupted write, a storage error — the game recovers to a safe state instead of crashing, and tells you. Settings and separate modes are preserved where possible. If this happens repeatedly, please email me; that is a bug, not normal.

I am stuck.

Use Hint. It points at a square you can work out right now from what is already on the board, rather than revealing the answer. Undo steps back one move and Restart clears the clearing.

When does Daily Glow unlock?

After you restore five gardens. It is one new puzzle each day, the same one for everyone, and finishing it on consecutive days builds a streak.

What is Endless Night?

It opens once you finish Moonbell Hollow, the first world. It builds fresh clearings on the spot at four depths — Drift, Dusk, Deep Night and Starless — and never runs out. The number beside each depth is how far the reasoning goes: 1–2 is pure deduction, 3 needs one supposition, 4 needs two, 5 needs three.

Can I turn off the sound, or the movement?

Yes. Settings has Sound, Haptics and Reduced Motion. Reduced Motion shortens or removes animation throughout, and the app also follows your system-wide reduce-motion setting.

Does it need the internet?

No. Firefly Garden works entirely offline and makes no network requests at all.

Is there anything to buy?

No. There are no purchases, no advertising, and no subscription.

Accessibility

Every control is labelled for VoiceOver and TalkBack, the board announces its size and each square's state, and no interactive element is smaller than the 44-point minimum touch target. Reduced Motion is honoured. If something is hard to reach or hard to hear, please tell me — that is a bug worth fixing.

Reporting a problem

Email sampetersen007@gmail.com with what happened, what you expected, the clearing's name, and your device and OS version. A screenshot helps.
