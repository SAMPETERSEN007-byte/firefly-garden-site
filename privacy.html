Firefly Garden

Privacy Policy

Firefly Garden collects nothing.

No account. No analytics. No advertising. No third-party SDKs. The app makes no network requests of its own, so there is nothing for us to receive.

Effective 31 August 2026 · applies to Firefly Garden for iOS and Android

The short version. Everything Firefly Garden knows about you — which gardens you have restored, your best move counts, your Daily Glow streak, and your sound and motion settings — is written to storage on your own device and never leaves it. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What is stored on your device

  • Which clearings you have unlocked and completed, and your best number of moves for each.
  • The board you are part-way through, so you can close the app and come back to it.
  • Your Daily Glow history and streak, and your Endless Night progress.
  • Your settings: sound, haptics, and reduced motion.

This is stored using the operating system's ordinary app storage. It is not readable by other apps, it is not synced to us, and no copy is sent anywhere.

What is not collected

  • No account. There is no sign-up, no login, and no email address.
  • No analytics or telemetry. The app contains no analytics SDK and sends no usage events.
  • No advertising and no ad identifiers. There are no ads, no tracking pixels, and no use of the advertising identifier.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera. The app never asks for these permissions because it never uses them.
  • No third-party SDKs of any kind, so nothing is shared with anyone.

What the app stores do see

Apple and Google run the stores the app is distributed through, and they report aggregate figures to us as the developer — roughly how many people downloaded the app, crash reports, and anonymous retention percentages. That collection is done by the platform, not by Firefly Garden, and it is governed by their policies, not this one. We cannot see who you are in any of it.

You can limit what your device shares with Apple under Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, and with Google under Settings → Google → Ads.

Children

Firefly Garden is a quiet logic puzzle suitable for any age. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Your data rights

Rights such as access, correction, portability, and erasure exist so you can act on data a company holds about you. We hold none. Everything the app has is already on your device and under your control: deleting the app erases it, and there is nothing for us to look up, export, or delete on your behalf.

If this ever changes

If a future version of Firefly Garden collects anything at all, this page will be updated before that version ships, the effective date at the top will change, and the change will be described here rather than quietly folded in.
