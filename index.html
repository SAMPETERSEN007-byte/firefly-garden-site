Firefly Garden

Place each glow. Wake every flower. Keep the fireflies apart.

How it plays

1
  1. Tap a square to place a firefly.
  2. Its light reaches every square it can see. Up, down, left and right, until a stone or a tree is in the way.
  3. No two fireflies can ever see each other.
  4. A number on a stone is how many fireflies sit right next to it.
  5. Light every square and the garden wakes up.

Stuck? A hint points to a square that has to hold a firefly, or to one you placed wrong.

Three ways to play

100 gardens

Four worlds with 25 gardens each. Moonbell Hollow, Thornwake Fen, Emberfall Wood and Starless Reach.

Daily Glow

A new puzzle every day. Same one for everyone. One try. Keep your streak alive.

Endless Night

It never runs out. The gardens get bigger and harder the deeper you go.

The Daily Glow and Endless Night have Game Center leaderboards. Play against everyone or just your friends.

Good to know

  • It's free. Your first 10 gardens have no ads. After that an ad shows up between gardens now and then, never in the middle of one.
  • Hints are free at first. After your first 10 gardens, you can watch an ad for a hint if you want one.
  • Remove Ads is one purchase. It's $5.99 in the US. It turns the ads off and makes hints free. It's not a subscription.
  • Tips are optional. They're just a way to say thanks.
  • No account. Your progress stays on your phone.
  • Plays offline. The Daily Glow checks the date online, and ads and purchases need a connection.
  • Made for everyone. It works with VoiceOver, Dynamic Type and Reduce Motion.
  • Leaderboards use Game Center. You don't need anything else.
