Place each glow. Wake every flower. Keep the fireflies apart.

One hundred hand-tuned clearings across four gardens, a new shared puzzle every day, and an endless night that never runs out. No accounts, no advertising, no purchases, and no internet required.

Every board has exactly one answer, and every one is proved solvable by ordinary reasoning before it ships — so you are never asked to guess. The difficulty on a clearing is measured, not decided: it is how deep the reasoning actually has to go.
