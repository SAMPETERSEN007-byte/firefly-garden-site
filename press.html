About the game

Firefly Garden is a quiet logic puzzle for iPhone. You place fireflies on a small garden so their light reaches every square, and no two fireflies can see each other. Numbered stones tell you how many fireflies sit right beside them. There are 100 gardens across four worlds, a new Daily Glow puzzle every day that's the same for everyone, and an Endless Night that gets bigger and harder the deeper you go. Every puzzle has exactly one answer and can be solved by logic alone. It's free with ads, with a one time Remove Ads purchase and optional tips. There's no account and no subscription.
